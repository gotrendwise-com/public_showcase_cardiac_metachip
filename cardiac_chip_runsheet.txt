[Step 1] Substrate Surface Preparation (Fused SiO2)
  Instructions:
    * Piranha clean: H2SO4:H2O2 (3:1) at 120°C for 15 minutes (remove organic residues).
    * Cascade DI water rinse until resistivity > 18.2 MOhm-cm.
    * Surface activation & hydration: Standard Clean SC-1 (NH4OH:H2O2:H2O 1:1:5 at 70°C for 10 min) to generate dense silanol (-SiOH) surface termination, followed by DI quick dump rinse.
    * Nitrogen spin-dry.
    * Dehydration bake: 180°C on hotplate for 10 minutes in vacuum.

[Step 2] Polycrystalline Silicon Thin-Film Deposition
  Equipment: LPCVD Horizontal Furnace
  Parameters:
    - temperature_celsius: 620.0
    - pressure_mtorr: 300.0
    - gas_sih4_sccm: 100.0
    - target_thickness_nm: 357.2
    - deposition_rate_nm_min: 10.0
    - process_time_min: 35.7
    - film_stress: Low tensile (< 100 MPa)

[Step 3] Deep-UV (193nm) Photolithography
  Equipment: Deep-UV Stepper (193nm ArF Immersion / Dry)
  Parameters:
    - bottom_antireflective_coating: BARC ARC-29A (65 nm)
    - spin_coat: 3000 rpm (resist thickness 350 nm)
    - pre_bake: 110°C for 90 seconds
    - exposure_dose_mJ_cm2: 24.5
    - post_exposure_bake: 115°C for 90 seconds
    - developer: TMAH 2.38% (MF-CD-26) puddle develop for 60 seconds

[Step 4] Inductively Coupled Plasma Reactive Ion Etching (ICP-RIE)
  Equipment: Oxford Instruments Plasmalab 100 ICP
  Parameters:
    - chemistry: Pseudo-Bosch anisotropic cryo-cooled / pulsed fluorocarbon
    - gas_sf6_sccm: 25.0
    - gas_c4f8_sccm: 50.0
    - gas_o2_sccm: 3.0
    - chamber_pressure_mtorr: 12.0
    - icp_coil_power_w: 800.0
    - rf_platen_power_w: 65.0
    - dc_bias_v: -120.0
    - electrode_temp_c: 15.0
    - etch_rate_nm_min: 180.0
    - nominal_etch_time_min: 1.98
    - sidewall_verticality: 89.8° ± 0.3°
    - endpoint_detection: Optical emission spectroscopy (OES) laser reflectometer

[Step 5] Resist Strip & Quality Metrology Checklist
  Equipment: Downstream O2 Ashing & CD-SEM
  Instructions:
    * O2 plasma ash: 500W, 300 sccm O2, 200 mTorr for 10 minutes (remove fluorocarbon polymer veil).
    * Wet solvent clean: EKC-265 or Remover PG at 65°C for 20 minutes.
    * Top-down CD-SEM inspection: Measure minimum width and pitch at 5 wafer locations (center, top, bottom, left, right).
    * Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM): Measure pillar height and surface roughness (Rq < 1.5 nm).
    * Optical spectrophotometer verification: Measure normal incidence reflection and transmission.

[Step 6] Biochemical Surface Functionalization (Troponin-I Immunoassay Preparation)
  Equipment: Biosafety Level 2 (BSL-2) Wet Chemistry Hood & Spin Coater
  Instructions:
    * Surface Silanization: Incubate metasurface in 2% (v/v) APTES in anhydrous ethanol for 1 hour to introduce amine (-NH2) functional groups.
    * Ethanol ultrasonic wash (3 cycles) and cure at 110°C for 30 minutes under N2.
    * Crosslinker Activation: Immerse in 2.5% Glutaraldehyde in PBS (pH 7.4) for 1 hour to create reactive aldehyde groups.
    * Antibody Immobilization: Incubate with 20 ug/mL monoclonal anti-cardiac Troponin-I (anti-cTnI) antibody solution at 4°C for 12 hours.
    * Surface Blocking: Incubate in 1% Bovine Serum Albumin (BSA) in PBS for 30 minutes to eliminate non-specific protein binding.
    * Storage & QA: Store in PBS with 0.05% NaN3 at 4°C until microfluidic integration or clinical sample exposure.
